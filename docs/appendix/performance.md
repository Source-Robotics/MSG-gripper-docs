# Performance tests

Thermal tests of the MSG gripper under different conditions. The thermal limit is 75 °C at the thermistor in the
motor windings.

!!! warning
    A gripper built without a thermistor in the windings has no temperature protection and can damage its motor
    in normal operation.

## Several days of cycling

- Open and close every 2.5 s, no load: the current near 0
- 7 days without interruption
- Motor temperature: 42 °C
- Ambient temperature: 33 °C

## Holding at a constant current

- Hold an object at a constant current for 24 hours
- Current setpoint: to be published
- Stable motor temperature: to be published
- Ambient temperature: to be published

## The current that trips the thermal limit

- Hold an object at a high current until the temperature error trips
- Current setpoint and the time to the error: to be published
- Ambient temperature: to be published

The results of the two open tests, and the measured force-against-current curves for the three steppers, will be
added here as they are made.
