# Guides

Worked examples around the gripper.

- [Build your own](build.md): print the parts for your rail and stepper, source the rest from the BOM, assemble,
  configure the driver.
- [Teleoperation with two grippers](teleoperation.md): one gripper follows the other and pushes back what it
  feels, over one CAN bus and a few lines of Python.

More examples, for the driver as much as for the gripper, are in the
[Spectral-BLDC-Python examples](https://github.com/Source-Robotics/Spectral-BLDC-Python/tree/main/examples) and in
the [STEPFOC guides](https://docs.source-robotics.com/STEPFOC-docs/latest/guides/).
